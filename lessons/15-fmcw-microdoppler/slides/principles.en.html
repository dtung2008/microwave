FMCW = frequency-modulated continuous wave

1.1 Two radars that cannot do the job

CW cannot range; pulse cannot listen close-in

CW (continuous wave): the echo carries range only as phase 4πR/λ — modulo 2π.

At 77 GHz: range known modulo 1.95 mm. Superb speedometer, useless rangefinder — zero bandwidth has no clock.

Pulse: can't listen while shouting. A 1 µs pulse blinds the receiver to cτ/2 = 150 m.

Our whole parking lot is inside 150 m. A 3 ns pulse fixes it — but that IS a 300 MHz signal, with detection energy crammed into peak power.

Close-in radar wants long-transmission energy with wide-bandwidth time-marking, transmitted and received simultaneously. That machine is FMCW.

1.1 Three claims for today

Three claims for today

  1. The mixer is the ranging engine.
    L12's dechirp mixer turns range into a beat tone: fb = 2Rαc/c. The receiver is a mixer plus an FFT (fast Fourier transform).
  2. Bandwidth is resolution: ΔR = c/2B.
    Power buys detection (L14); bandwidth buys separation. 300 MHz separates half-meters at any transmit power.
  3. The second FFT reads velocity; the long stare reads intent.
    Chirp-to-chirp phase spin → the range-Doppler map. An 80 ms stare → the micro-Doppler comb that tells a drone from a bird.
1.2 The chirp on the f–t plane

Two parallel ramps, one lag

αc = B/Tc = 3×10¹³ Hz/s

The vertical gap between the ramps is constant: αcτ. Range is a frequency waiting to be measured.

1.2 The dechirp receiver — L12's hardware, promoted

The LO (local oscillator) is the waveform; the mixer is the ranging engine

fb = αc·τ = 2Rαc/c = 200.14 kHz per meter

drone at 25 m → 5.0 MHz · airliner tail at 180 m → 36.06 MHz. A 300 MHz-wide problem arrives at the ADC (analog-to-digital converter) as a tone. FMCW is the poor engineer's matched filter — and why a 77 GHz radar costs like a WiFi chip. (PLL = phase-locked loop, L12's synthesizer.)

1.3 The dechirp, honestly

The full phase — three terms, all of them features

Δφ(t) = 2π[ f₀τ  +  αcτ·t  −  ½αcτ² ] ,   τ = 2R/c
  • f₀τ = 4πR/λ / 2π — the carrier phase. Constant within a chirp; across chirps it spins at the Doppler rate — hour 2 lives here.
  • αcτ·t — the beat tone, fb = 2Rαc/c. The fast derivation, recovered.
  • ½αcτ² — residual video phase: a few hundred Hz of constant offset at 180 m. Negligible today; the toolkit keeps it anyway (honesty is cheaper than remembering what you dropped).

The homework's channel simulator is only this expression, evaluated at every sample instant, with τ = 2r(t)/c. Coupling, migration, blade modulation — all fall out of the geometry.

1.3 The R–v coupling

One chirp cannot split range from velocity

fbeat = 2Rαc/c + 2v/λ   (v = range rate, receding +; course-wide sign convention)

The Doppler shift rides on the beat. Car receding at 20 m/s: apparent range +51.3 mm (measured +46.8 by interpolated FFT). At 77 GHz the car's 6 kHz of Doppler perturbs a 9 MHz beat by 0.07% — but that 0.07% is 100% of the velocity information.

Self-protecting waveform: coupling in bins = fdTc = 2vTc/λ, and vunamb = λ/4Tc ⟹ worst case inside the unambiguous window = exactly ½ range bin (250 mm here) — for any chirp-sequence waveform. Homework Q5.

1.4 Range resolution

The FFT bin is the resolution — and Tc cancels

  1. One chirp is a Tc-long record → FFT bins are 1/Tc wide (the uncertainty principle in engineering clothes).
  2. Two targets separate when beats differ by one bin:
    2·ΔR·αc/c ≥ 1/Tc
  3. Substitute αc = B/Tc — Tc cancels:
ΔR = c/2B = 0.4997 m at 300 MHz

Bandwidth is resolution. Power buys detection range (L14); bandwidth buys separation. Different currencies.

The 77–81 GHz automotive band offers 4 GHz → 3.7 cm — why regulators gave cars that band, and why your car sees the curb.

2.1 Doppler, chirp to chirp

The carrier-phase term earns its keep

fd = 2v/λ = 513.7 Hz per m/s at 77 GHz  (car at 11.8 m/s → 6.06 kHz · blade tip at 69 m/s → 35.5 kHz)
  • Each chirp carries phase term f₀τn = 4πRn/λ (over 2π). Chirp to chirp, R moves by vTc → the beat's phase spins at exactly fd.
  • Fast time (512 samples in a chirp): FFT → range columns.
  • Slow time (512 chirps): FFT at each range bin → Doppler rows, sampled at PRF (pulse/chirp repetition frequency) = 1/Tc = 100 kHz.

Sign convention, fixed once for the course: v = range rate, receding positive (L16's collision logic wants range rate anyway). The physics — closing targets shift the carrier up — is untouched; the toolkit's axis carries the sign.

2.1 The chirp-sequence processing

Two FFTs, physical axes — the modern radar's retina

One CPI (coherent processing interval) = N·Tc = 5.12 ms → a full 512×512 map: range × range-rate, in meters and m/s.

2.1 The Doppler axis

Same theorem, second dimension

Resolution — the record lasts NTc:

Δv = λ/2NTc = 0.3802 m/s

Ambiguity — slow time is sampled at PRF:

vunamb = ±λ/4Tc = ±97.3 m/s
  • Record length sets the bin; sample rate sets the window. L12 said "sampling is a mixer too" — here it prices what the radar can see.
  • Bonus ceiling: a target must stay in one range cell per CPI → ΔR/CPI = 97.6 m/s. Nothing in this design is accidental.
2.2 The waveform-design triangle

Three knobs, four demands

specconstraint
ΔR ≤ 0.5 mB ≥ 299.79 MHz
coverage ≥ 200 mTc ≥ 7.82 µs
vunamb ≥ ±75 m/sTc ≤ 12.98 µs
Δv ≤ 0.4 m/sN ≥ 486.7

Tc is squeezed from both sides — legal window [7.82, 12.98] µs. Pick 300 MHz / 10 µs / 512.

2.2 The spec's odd number

±75 m/s in a parking lot? — the blades wrote the spec

  • No car in a parking lot does 270 km/h. The vunamb ≥ ±75 m/s line exists because blade tips do 69 m/s (next section) — the customer wants the micro-Doppler comb unaliased and observable.
  • The spec drives the waveform; the target drives the spec.

War story — the wind farm. A surveillance radar's PRF gives a few tens of m/s of unambiguous velocity. A turbine's blade disc smears −100 to +100 m/s tip-to-tip — a 200 m/s-wide Doppler ribbon folding over and over into the radar's window, every scan, 40+ dB over noise: rain that never moves, aircraft that don't exist. Several countries now negotiate turbine siting against radar coverage maps. One triangle, drawn badly at national scale.

2.3 Micro-Doppler — the fast version

A rotating blade phase-modulates the echo

Carrier phase modulation index:

β = 4πL/λ = 355 rad

Sinusoidal phase of index β → Bessel sidebands Jn(β) out to n ≈ β (the FM-radio theorem) → a band of half-width β·frot = 2vtip/λ = 35.5 kHz.

vtip = 2πfrotL = 69.1 m/s — the blade waves at the radar and the radar hears FM.

2.3 Micro-Doppler — first principles

Chen's rotating scatterer [R24] — no hand-waving left

  • Put a point scatterer at the tip, write τ(t) = 2r(t)/c into hour 1's phase expansion — done. Instantaneous slow-time frequency:
f(t) = 2r(t)/λ = −(2vtip/λ)·sin(2πfrott)
  • A sinusoid in the spectrogram, swinging ±35.5 kHz — that sinusoid is micro-Doppler. Chen 2006 §II builds every rotating, vibrating, tumbling target from this one scatterer (course pack; four equations).
  • The homework's channel simulator contains nothing else: point scatterers whose r(t) you choose. The spectrogram falls out of the same phase that made the beat tone.
2.3 The HERM comb

Periodicity makes lines; symmetry decimates them

One blade repeats every 1/frot → comb at frot. Nb identical, evenly spaced blades repeat every 1/(Nbfrot) →

ΔfHERM = Nb·frot = 200 Hz  (HERM = helicopter rotor modulation — Vietnam-era jargon, never improved)

2.3 The payoff

Same blip, different machinery

Drone (−20 dBsm)

  • comb spaced Nbfrot = 200 Hz → a mechanical fingerprint
  • band edge 2vtip/λ = 35.5 kHz → propeller-sized tips
  • blade flash every 1/(Nbfrot) = 5 ms in the STFT (short-time Fourier transform)

Bird (also −20 dBsm)

  • body Doppler + few-Hz wingbeat flutter
  • no comb, no wide band edge — wingtips do m/s, not 69 m/s

Lecture 1's debt paid: the drone and the bird were the same −20 dBsm blip; the RCS (radar cross section) never separates them — the spectrogram does. This is classification as physics; the machine-learning drone-detection literature [R28][R29] eats these features.

Honest footnotes: real quads dither the four rotor RPMs (each line splits into a cluster; the spacing survives). Our waveform observes the comb unaliased — L14's radar could not have (its Doppler window was narrower than one blade sweep: the wind farm in miniature).

2.4 The industrial instance [R34]

TI's (Texas Instruments) 77 GHz single chip — this lecture, on one die

  • Up to 4 GHz of ramp across 76–81 GHz (ΔR → 3.7 cm); IF = intermediate frequency, band-limited so the ADC digitizes beats, not RF — §1.2's dividend, productized.

Hour 2 in four sentences
