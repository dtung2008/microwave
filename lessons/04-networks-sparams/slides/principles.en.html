1.1 Why this lecture is the hinge

Three claims for today

  1. Voltage and current stop being good coordinates.
    Not wrong — ambiguous. Traveling-wave amplitudes are what instruments measure.
  2. Representations are interchangeable; algebra is not.
    S, Z, ABCD convert freely — but cascade with the wrong one and you get confident nonsense.
  3. Physics referees every S-matrix.
    Reciprocity · unitarity · passivity: one-number tests. Tonight, three files — at least one is lying.
1.2 Why voltage loses its meaning

Which voltage is "the" port voltage?

Lecture 2's standing wave: V(z) = V⁺(e−jβz + Γe+jβz)

  • |V| swings between |V⁺|(1+|Γ|) and |V⁺|(1−|Γ|)
  • At SWR (standing wave ratio) = 3: a 3:1 disagreement depending on probe position
  • At 10 GHz the answer changes every few mm

Voltage still exists — it is just a property of the position, not of the port.

1.2 Why voltage loses its meaning

The classical matrices need hardware that does not exist

  • Z-parameters need open circuits (I = 0) — but a microwave "open" radiates and looks capacitive
  • Y-parameters need shorts (V = 0) — but a "short" is a via with inductance
  • Active devices asked to drive an open at resonance answer with an oscillation

War story: a 6 GHz transistor "characterized" with a dangling SMA connector as the open standard. Beautiful smooth curves — none of them of the transistor. The connector was a quarter-wave stub: the intended open was a near-short, and the DUT (device under test) spent the sweep two ohms from oscillating.

What is well-defined everywhere: the two traveling waves. Terminate everything in 50 Ω, measure waves — that is the S-parameter program.

1.3 Wave amplitudes, derived twice

a and b — voltage bookkeeping becomes energy bookkeeping

a = (V + Z₀I) / (2√Z₀)    b = (V − Z₀I) / (2√Z₀)

a = incident (into the network), b = outgoing; Z₀ = reference impedance, real 50 Ω all course; V, I are RMS (root-mean-square) phasors

Why the √Z₀: compute the power into the port — P = Re(V·I*) = |a|² − |b|².
|a|² is incident power in watts, |b|² outgoing. Scattering coefficients will compare energies, not volts.

Inverse: V = √Z₀(a + b), I = (a − b)/√Z₀. Matched line: b = 0, V/I = Z₀ — wave picture and circuit picture agree exactly where they should.

1.3 Wave amplitudes, derived twice

Nothing is new — Γ got promoted

b/a = (ZL − Z₀)/(ZL + Z₀) = Γ
  • The reflection coefficient of lecture 2 is the S₁₁ of a one-port
  • The Smith chart of lecture 3 is the complex plane of b/a
  • Every port gets its own a pointing in, b pointing out — a two-port has four numbers per frequency

Continuity check you can hold onto: everything you learned about Γ — magnitude ≤ 1 for passive loads, chart rotations, SWR — is about to generalize to matrices.

1.4 The S-matrix

b = S a — and what "a₂ = 0" really means

b₁b₂ = S₁₁S₁₂S₂₁S₂₂ a₁a₂     S₁₁ = b₁/a₁ |a₂=0

a₂ = 0 means port 2 terminated in Z₀ — not open. You zero a wave with a matched load. And S₁₁ is the input reflection only when port 2 is matched; with a load ΓL, Γin = S₁₁ + S₁₂S₂₁ΓL/(1 − S₂₂ΓL) — hour 2 derives it. Misreading S₁₁ as "the match" is the field's most common datasheet mistake.

All S entries are referenced to 50 Ω here unless a file says otherwise — and the file saying otherwise matters.

1.4 The S-matrix

The VNA in one slide — what those two cables do

  • A directional coupler (built in L7) physically separates the wave going in from the wave coming out
  • Ratio two phasors → S₁₁ measured directly. No opens, no shorts — everything 50 Ω, always
  • Calibration (short/open/load/thru standards) moves the reference plane from the VNA's connectors to yours

S-parameters are the two-port language rebuilt around the one operation microwave does well: terminate and measure waves.

1.4 The S-matrix

Reading the four entries

S₁₁ — input match

lecture 3's whole subject, now one matrix entry

S₂₁ — transmission

the gain or loss of the thing (into matched loads!)

S₁₂ — reverse transmission

isolation when small; feedback when it matters (L11: instability lives here)

S₂₂ — output match

what the next stage sees looking back

Vocabulary you will use forever: |S₁₁| in dB = return loss · |S₂₁| in dB = insertion loss (sign flipped) · |S₁₂| in dB = isolation.

1.6 Recap

Hour 1 in three sentences

  • Voltage on a line is a function of position — ports speak in waves: a in, b out, |·|² in watts.
  • The S-matrix is b = S a, every zero enforced by a matched load — which is why a VNA can actually measure it.
  • Lossless devices print |S₂₁| < 1 all the time — reflection is not dissipation.

2.1 The matrix zoo

Conversions — free to move, refereed to 10⁻¹⁰

Z = Z₀(I − S)⁻¹(I + S)     S = (Z − Z₀I)(Z + Z₀I)⁻¹

matrix forms, any number of ports (I = identity). S ↔ ABCD: the 2-port formulas with 2S₂₁ in every denominator — slide notes, starter docstrings, Pozar Table 4.2.

Homework module 1: implement all four + cascade; scikit-rf (s2a / a2s / s2z / z2s) is the referee. Syllabus bar 10⁻¹⁰; the reference solution measures 1.6·10⁻¹².

Transcribing conversion formulas correctly once is exactly the work you delegate to an AI — and then verify against the referee. That division of labor is the course's working model.

2.2 Cascading

Why S-matrices do not multiply

  • S maps incident → outgoing. S·S′ wires outputs to outputs — an unphysical question
  • Route 1: convert to ABCD, multiply, convert back (skrf's **; homework's cascade)
  • Route 2: sum the bounces — S₂₁,tot = S₂₁′S₂₁/(1 − S₂₂S₁₁′) — the 1/(1−x) is the geometric series

Matched interfaces (S₂₂S₁₁′ → 0) kill the bounces — which is why matching makes cascade design sane.

Honest footnote: the naive product of two lossless S-matrices is still unitary — the unitarity check alone would miss this. You need the whole suite.

2.3 The invariants

Reciprocity: S = Sᵀ

  • Lorentz reciprocity: any network of linear, isotropic materials — metal, dielectric, lossy or not — transmits identically both ways: S₂₁ = S₁₂ always, however asymmetric the layout looks
  • What breaks it: magnetized ferrites (isolators, circulators), plasmas, and active devices — a transistor's whole job is S₂₁ ≫ S₁₂
  • So a reciprocity check doubles as an activity detector

Residual: max |S − Sᵀ| over the band. Plain transpose — no conjugate. (Sᴴ belongs to the next two invariants; mixing the transposes is the homework's classic bug.)

2.3 The invariants

Losslessness: SᴴS = I — proved, then instrumented

Fast: lossless ⇒ ‖b‖² = ‖a‖² for every excitation ⇒ ‖Sa‖² = ‖a‖² for all a ⇒ S unitary.

First-principles: ‖a‖² − ‖Sa‖² = aᴴ(I − SᴴS)a — a quadratic form vanishing for all a forces I − SᴴS = 0, term by term.

Column 1 reads |S₁₁|² + |S₂₁|² = 1: the λ/4 line (0.3846² + 0.9231² = 1.0000) and the transformer (0.36 + 0.64) pass on the board.

Unitarity residual = max over f of ‖SᴴS − I‖ (Frobenius norm).

×½ pad: SᴴS = ¼I ⇒ residual = ¾√2 = 1.06066
lossless line: 10⁻¹⁶
ring_slot (measured file): 0.078 — it radiates

One number, and "lossless" stops being a marketing word. Tonight: a file that claims lossless scores 0.030.

2.3 The invariants

Passivity: no singular value above 1

‖a‖² − ‖Sa‖² ≥ 0 for every a  ⇔  I − SᴴS ⪰ 0  ⇔  σmax(S) ≤ 1
  • A passive network may dissipate but may not emit
  • Singular values, not eigenvalues — S is not Hermitian; eigenvalues of a non-normal matrix can sit inside the unit circle while the network pumps power. SVD (singular value decomposition) does the honest bookkeeping
  • Residual: max over f of max(σmax² − 1, 0). Zero for every honest passive file; positive means active hardware or broken data — no third option
2.3 The invariants

The vote: can a passive network show |S₂₁| > 1?

No — not in a real reference impedance, not at resonance, not ever. σmax ≤ 1 bounds every entry. The λ/4 line doubles voltage across a high-Z load — real physics, but the √Z₀ in a and b already converted the books to energy, and passive devices have no energy to add.

What can legitimately exceed 1: voltage transfer functions V₂/V₁, and pseudo-wave S-parameters referenced to complex Z₀. Neither lives in a 50 Ω file. Tonight one planted file prints |S₂₁| = +8 dB — the "resonance can do it" voters would have acquitted an amplifier.

Tolerances are engineering: measured residuals are never zero — the planted "VNA" noise (~10⁻⁴/entry) floors them near 10⁻³; course tolerances sit ~5× above the floor, with the rationale written next to the constants. A tolerance nobody can defend is a tolerance somebody will quietly widen.

2.4 Reference planes

De-embedding — and the invariants' blind spot

S′ = diag(e−jβℓ₁, e−jβℓ₂) · S · diag(e−jβℓ₁, e−jβℓ₂)
  • S-parameters are defined at planes; moving a plane by ℓ multiplies waves by e−jβℓ. De-embedding = choosing ℓ to strip the fixture
  • Phases are meaningless until you know where the planes are

The shift matrix is unitary — reciprocity, unitarity, and passivity are all blind to a reference-plane error. The suite convicts liars, not sloppy bookkeepers. Know which enemy you are hunting.

2.4 Signal-flow graphs

Mason lite: the one-loop rule

Γin = S₁₁ + S₁₂S₂₁ΓL / (1 − S₂₂ΓL)
  • ΓL = 0 → S₁₁ (the matched definition, recovered)
  • S₁₂ = 0 → S₁₁ (no feedback path — the load becomes invisible)

Lecture 11 puts a transistor in the box and derives every gain formula from this same graph. These ten minutes are an investment.
