# Course references

Numbered, append-only. Keys are stable: never renumber, never delete; append at the end.
Free links preferred; entries without links have no legal free copy (course-pack via
institutional access; never link mirrors). Verified 2026-08-04 where a link is given.

## Texts

- [R1] D. M. Pozar, *Microwave Engineering*, 4th ed., Wiley, 2011. The course's structural
  backbone and the "buy one book" option.
- [R2] M. Steer, *Microwave and RF Design*, 3rd ed., Vols. 1–5, NC State Univ. / UNC Press,
  2019. **Free (open access):** <https://repository.lib.ncsu.edu/handle/1840.20/36776>;
  HTML: LibreTexts "Microwave and RF Design I–V (Steer)". The free co-primary text.
- [R3] M. Steer, *Fundamentals of Microwave and RF Design*, 3rd ed., NC State Univ., 2019.
  Free, same repository; single-volume condensation of [R2].
- [R4] S. J. Orfanidis, *Electromagnetic Waves and Antennas*, Rutgers Univ., 2016 (rev.).
  **Free (author-licensed):** <https://www.ece.rutgers.edu/~orfanidi/ewa/>. EM/antenna
  companion with runnable MATLAB.
- [R5] R. Ludwig and G. Bogdanov, *RF Circuit Design: Theory and Applications*, 2nd ed.,
  Pearson, 2009. Gentler on-ramp for Smith chart and matching.
- [R6] R. E. Collin, *Foundations for Microwave Engineering*, 2nd ed., IEEE Press/Wiley,
  2001. Rigor reference (waveguide/mode theory).
- [R7] G. Gonzalez, *Microwave Transistor Amplifiers: Analysis and Design*, 2nd ed.,
  Prentice Hall, 1997. Amplifier-design lectures reference.
- [R8] S. Ramo, J. R. Whinnery, T. Van Duzer, *Fields and Waves in Communication
  Electronics*, 3rd ed., Wiley, 1994. Fields-to-circuits background.
- [R9] F. T. Ulaby and U. Ravaioli, *Fundamentals of Applied Electromagnetics*, 8th ed.,
  Pearson, 2022. Undergrad EM refresher pointer.
- [R10] C. A. Balanis, *Balanis' Advanced Engineering Electromagnetics*, 3rd ed., Wiley,
  2024. Grad EM reference; RCS-reduction/metasurface chapter cited in the radar thread.
- [R11] C. A. Balanis, *Antenna Theory: Analysis and Design*, 4th ed., Wiley, 2016.
  Antenna/array lecture reference.
- [R12] M. I. Skolnik, *Introduction to Radar Systems*, 3rd ed., McGraw-Hill, 2001.
- [R13] M. A. Richards, J. A. Scheer, W. A. Holm (eds.), *Principles of Modern Radar,
  Vol. I: Basic Principles*, SciTech/IET, 2010. The modern classroom radar text.
- [R14] M. A. Richards, *Fundamentals of Radar Signal Processing*, 2nd ed., McGraw-Hill,
  2014. Doppler, pulse compression, CFAR mathematics.
- [R15] B. R. Mahafza, *Radar Systems Analysis and Design Using MATLAB*, 4th ed., CRC
  Press, 2022.
- [R16] V. C. Chen, *The Micro-Doppler Effect in Radar*, 2nd ed., Artech House, 2019.
- [R17] R. J. Mailloux, *Phased Array Antenna Handbook*, 3rd ed., Artech House, 2017.
- [R18] G. Matthaei, L. Young, E. M. T. Jones, *Microwave Filters, Impedance-Matching
  Networks, and Coupling Structures*, Artech House, 1980 (orig. McGraw-Hill 1964).
